## Diagnosis

The saveable route should survive process death, so the code I read does not clearly explain the Shop landing, and I could not confirm the mechanism from source alone. What is certain is that the DetailScreen has no form inputs (DetailScreen.kt:90-113), so the 'fill' step left no state to lose, and that the product is re-fetched after restore through a 5-second delay (ProductRepository.kt:51-52) that the DetailScreen shows only as a spinner. Confidence is low; the next step is to check the saved-state bundle or the restored route directly.
Fix approach: Move the route into a ViewModel whose state is held in SavedStateHandle (or keep it in rememberSaveable with an explicit Saver), so the back stack is restored deterministically. Tie the product load to the ViewModel scope instead of GlobalScope in DealLoader, so a restored Detail screen reloads its data without depending on the composition or the activity instance.
