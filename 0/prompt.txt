droid-monkey attacked this Android app with a real-world fault and it failed. Fix the root cause.

## What happened
Attack `process_death` (process_death): Fill the screen's form, let Android kill the app in the background, then come back to it.
Steps: [{"fill": true}, {"fault": "kill_in_background"}, {"fault": "relaunch"}, {"settle": 6}]
Result: lost_state: 6 of 6 filled fields changed or emptied
Fields lost (before -> after): {'field_name': ['Ravi Kumar', ''], 'field_email': ['ravi@example.com', ''], 'field_phone': ['9876543210', ''], 'field_address': ['12 MG Road', ''], 'field_city': ['Bengaluru', ''], 'field_pincode': ['560001', '']}

## Diagnosis
Root cause: The six delivery fields are plain `mutableStateOf` properties on `CheckoutViewModel` (CheckoutViewModel.kt:14-19), and the ViewModel has no constructor parameter for `SavedStateHandle`. ViewModel state survives only configuration changes, not process death. When the app is killed in the background, the ViewModel is discarded, and on relaunch `CheckoutScreen`'s `viewModel()` call (CheckoutScreen.kt:34) builds a fresh instance with empty strings, so every field reads as blank (CheckoutScreen.kt:67-72). The screen has no other persisted copy of the input, so the user's entries are lost, which matches the six emptied fields in the verdict.
Fix approach: Make the form state survive process death by moving it into a SavedStateHandle. Change CheckoutViewModel to take a SavedStateHandle and back each field with `savedStateHandle.getStateFlow` or `saveable` state, keyed by field name, so values are written to the saved-state bundle. Keep `isComplete` and `placeOrder` unchanged, and keep the `viewModel()` call in CheckoutScreen, which injects SavedStateHandle by default. Optionally use rememberSaveable for the field values in the composable if the ViewModel should stay simple.
Evidence: app/src/main/java/com/shubhasai/monkeymart/ui/CheckoutViewModel.kt:13 - CheckoutViewModel is a plain ViewModel() with no SavedStateHandle constructor parameter.; app/src/main/java/com/shubhasai/monkeymart/ui/CheckoutViewModel.kt:14-19 - all six fields (name, email, phone, address, city, pincode) are `mutableStateOf("")` with no saved-state backing, so they are lost when the process is killed.; app/src/main/java/com/shubhasai/monkeymart/ui/CheckoutScreen.kt:34 - `vm: CheckoutViewModel = viewModel()` creates a fresh ViewModel on relaunch, so the form starts empty.; app/src/main/java/com/shubhasai/monkeymart/ui/CheckoutScreen.kt:67-72 - the six CheckoutField calls bind directly to the ViewModel's in-memory properties, with no other persisted copy of the input.; verdict - 6 of 6 filled fields changed from the entered values to '' after kill_in_background and relaunch.
