## Diagnosis
Root cause: CouponScanner remembers camera access in a SharedPreferences boolean that is set once after a grant (CouponScanner.kt:37, written in ScanCouponButton at :66) and never cleared. The Scan button gates on that flag alone (CouponScanner.kt:76-77), so after Settings revokes CAMERA the app still believes it has access and calls CameraManager.openCamera directly (CouponScanner.kt:44), which throws SecurityException. The fault invalidates the cached grant, but the app never rechecks the real permission state.
Fix approach: Drop the persisted flag and make the gate ask the system: use ContextCompat.checkSelfPermission(context, Manifest.permission.CAMERA) == PERMISSION_GRANTED in hasCameraAccess. If it is not granted, launch the RequestPermission contract instead of opening the camera. Also wrap openCamera in try/catch for SecurityException and surface a status message as a last-resort guard against races with revocation.
